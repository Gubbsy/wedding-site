Mains

Mains will consist of a mexican feast with chilli con carne and rice, chicken nachos and mexican roast sweet potatoes

Chilli is Adam's favourite food, if you don't like what is on offer, there is no other option, we won't be offended if you bring a pack lunch

Evening Meal

Between 7pm and 9pm a hot dog stand will be providing evening stomach fillers to help keep you going

Allergies and Dietary Requirements

All food options can be made Veggie/Vegan or Gluten free upon request, these requirements will need to be captured by END OF NOVEMBER 2024. Please place any dietary requests through the contact section of our website here.

Drinks

We will provide glasses of Prosecco immediately after the service. Wine will be on the table during the evening meal, and a bar will be open following the meal, fully stocked, including cocktails.
