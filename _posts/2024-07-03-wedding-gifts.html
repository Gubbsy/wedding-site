HOPE Not Hate

HOPE Not Hate is an advocacy group based in the UK, which campaigns against racism and fascism.

It is self described as 'non-partisan, non-sectarian organisation'. Their team is made up of researchers, educators, community activists, policy experts, and specialists with inside access to far right groups.

Their work focuses on communities that are susceptible to far-right ideologies, and the issues and policies that give rise to them. They build skills and resilience across the communities and civil society organisations, creating an alternative narrative of togetherness and unity.

Cancer Research

Both of our lives and families have been effected by cancer.

Cancer Research studies in areas of: basic, population and clinical research. It aims to develop methods to prevent, detect, diagnose, treat and cure cancer.

Cancer Research is important because it helps us understand the biological processes involved in cancer, which has led to more effective treatments and prevention strategies
